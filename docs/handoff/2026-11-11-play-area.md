# Handoff

- Date. 2026-11-11.
- Branch and pull request. `cursor/play-area-drag-cfe0`. The pull request is the play-area change.
- What landed. You drag a hand card into the play area to stage it, and you drag it back to unstage it. The card lifts and grows while it moves, and the play area highlights when it can receive the drop. X sends every staged card back. Play stays on screen and stays disabled until the staged cards are a new meld or a layoff onto exactly one meld. That check is `PlayArea.offer(in:)`, which calls `HandState`. VoiceOver can stage with "Move to play area" and return one card with "Return to hand". A finger tap on a hand card still selects it for Lay down, Discard, and Move. The choice is `docs/adr/0012-play-area.md`.
- What is not done. Tapping the stock or the discard to draw, and dragging a card onto the discard, belong to the next ticket. `CardDragRoute` is the place to add those drop zones. Move left and Move right stay.
- How to check. `./scripts/check.sh` runs the engine tests. The iPhone simulator job runs `PlayAreaTests` and `testDragThreeHeartsAndPlayLaysDownTheRun`.
- Open questions. They stay in `docs/RULES.md`. This change does not answer them.
